# 🔐 Cybersecurity Compliance & GRC Fundamentals — Week 4

> Framework selection, privacy law alignment, and GRC thinking applied to a real-world business case.

---

## Overview

This project documents my Week 4 learning from the **SCWIST Cybersecurity Program (2026)**, focused on cybersecurity compliance, privacy law, and GRC frameworks.

The core deliverable is a framework selection and business case for **BlinkSend**, a fictional Canadian fintech startup. The task mirrors real work performed in entry-level GRC and compliance roles: read an organization's risk profile, pick the right framework, and make the business case for it.

---

## What This Project Covers

- What cybersecurity compliance is and what it is not
- Components of a cybersecurity program
- Key frameworks: NIST CSF, ISO/IEC 27001, CyberSecure Canada
- Canadian privacy laws: PIPEDA and Law 25 (Quebec)
- How to select the right framework for an organization
- Applying GRC thinking to a realistic fintech scenario
