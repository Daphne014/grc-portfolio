# Ontario Health Privacy Breach Analysis
## A GRC Compliance Case Study | Lakeshore Health Technologies

**Author:** Daphne Obadan | Compliance & GRC Professional | Toronto, ON
**Legislation:** PHIPA (Ontario) | PIPEDA (Federal)
**Anchor case:** PHIPA Decision 266, IPC Ontario, November 22, 2024

---

## What This Is

In November 2024, Ontario's IPC published a finding against a health clinic that improperly disposed of patient records and took over nine months to notify 482 affected patients.

This project takes that real enforcement decision and uses it to assess what the same legal failures would look like inside a digital health tech company. Lakeshore Health Technologies is the fictional company used as that lens, a cloud platform managing PHI for GTA family clinics. The law, the breach, and the regulatory reasoning are all real.
